# Raen Browser: privacy and terms

Updated 19 November 2026.

## Privacy

### What stays on this PC

- Your channels, groups, settings, Watch later, history and what you have watched are saved in a folder on this computer. They are not sent to us.
- Your sign-ins to the sites you use in the app (YouTube, X, Instagram, Twitch and the rest) are kept by the app's own browser, on this computer, the way any browser keeps them.
- Saved passwords are in an encrypted vault on this computer, locked by a master password only you know. Clipboard history, downloads and the Library are on this computer too.
- There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in Raen Browser, and we do not sell or share data about you.

### What goes to the rooms server

- Rooms, friends and messages work through a server we run (on Cloudflare). To do that it holds: your friend code, the name and picture you show, who your friends are, which rooms you are in, and whether you are online.
- Room chat, direct messages and the things shared in a room are encrypted on your PC before they are sent, and are stored on the server in that form.
- If you turn on sharing what you are watching or listening to, your friends see it (the title, not your history). You can turn it off, or appear offline, at any time.
- Your film log (what you have watched, your ratings, diary, reviews and lists) stays on this PC. If you use friends, a short summary goes to the server for your friends to see, unless you turn it off (Your films, Friends, Stop sharing): how many titles you have watched, your four favourites, and your latest 20 or so watches, ratings and reviews. It is stored there as it is (not encrypted like messages), only your friends are given it, and it is removed when you turn sharing off.
- Leaving a room or removing a friend removes you from it. To have a room or your mailbox deleted from the server, ask us.

### What goes to other services

- The sites you open: when you use a site inside the app, that site sees you as it would in any browser, under its own privacy policy.
- YouTube: the public build reads your subscriptions and likes from YouTube's own site, signed in as you. Nothing about them is sent to us.
- Spotify and TMDB: if you add your own keys, the app talks to those services directly with them.
- Ad blocking (optional): the block lists are downloaded from Ghostery. The Twitch ads option (off unless you turn it on) sends the request for a stream's playlist through a community-run server, which can see which channels you watch.
- AI panel: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in the side panel is that company's own site, under your account with them and their privacy policy. What you put in its message box (text you highlight, a page's or tab's text, a picture of part of the screen) goes to that company when you send it. The app sends nothing to an AI by itself and keeps no copy. Read aloud uses the voices on your PC and sends nothing anywhere.
- Updates: the app asks GitHub whether there is a new version.
